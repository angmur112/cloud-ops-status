Operations Procedure

Maintenance Runbook

Use this checklist to plan, execute, and verify a controlled maintenance window.

Prepare

  • Confirm the change owner and rollback decision-maker.
  • Record the expected customer impact.
  • Verify that a tested rollback path exists.

Execute

  • Announce the maintenance window.
  • Apply one controlled change at a time.
  • Record timestamps and observed results.

Recover

  • Run service health checks.
  • Roll back if the success criteria are not met.
  • Update the status dashboard and close the event.

Return to service status

Keep operational response paths versioned beside the service they support.
